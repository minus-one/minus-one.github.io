Bio
===

Adithya received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Pennsylvania State University in 2022, and his B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from NIT Trichy.
He worked at Meta from 2022-2026 before joining IIT Gandhinagar as an Assistant Professor. 

Research Interests
=================

Distributed Systems, AI/ML Infrastructure, Performance Evaluation
